# Bench session, 2026-09-26

Board A rev A, ST-LINK V2-1 `0671FF565357657287183750`, probe-rs 0.32.0,
equipment disconnected, board B not attached. The board started on the
8 KB layout (main's bootloader at `0x08000000`, its application at
`0x08002000`) and was moved to #185's 32 KB layout. The images were
d837e43's: `o89-boot.bin` SHA-256 `16c84b1278ad85ed…`, 448 bytes;
`o89-controller.bin` `f81d1be5514613e0…`, 244,280 bytes.

## The option bytes

`FLASH_OPTR` read `0xFFFFFEAA` before any flash and after the last one
(`just check-option-bytes`): `nSWAP_BANK` 1, `DUAL_BANK` 1, RDP level 0
(`0xAA`), `nBOOT_SEL` 1 with `nBOOT0` 1. With `nSWAP_BANK` at 1 the part ran
the bootloader at `0x08000000`, so 1 maps bank 1 at the bottom, as #185's
layout needs.

## The migration

1. `just flash-controller` on the 8 KB board refused before writing:
   `boot-matches: the bootloader on the part is not this checkout's
   o89-boot`. `0x08002000` still read `20022000 08002101` and `0x08008000`
   `2006d101 48c4e036`, the old image's bytes, afterwards.
2. `just flash-boot` wrote the 32 KB layout's bootloader. The word at
   `0x08008000` is not a stack pointer in RAM, so the bootloader waited with
   `RUN` and `KICK` low, as it should.
3. `just flash-controller` then passed the check, wrote the application at
   `0x08008000` and reset the part. `0x08000000` read `20024000 080000c1`
   and `0x08008000` read `20022000 08008101`.
4. `just attach-controller` for 45 s from a probe reset: the boot record
   (`cause: Software`, `revision: A`), the store (boot count 21), the rail
   settled and `EN` released, the NOR identified (`EF 40 18`), the ring open
   at block 50, the link up to the module (`fw 0.0.0+g56f2ce49`), the
   generator contact `Open`. After the attach, `DHCSR` read `01040001` and
   `DEMCR` 0: running, no catch left armed.

## The bootloader's wait hid the part from the probe

With no application behind it, the first build of the bootloader waited in
`wfi`. In that state `DHCSR` read `01040001` (`S_SLEEP`) and `CPUID`
`410cc601`, but every read over the bus returned zero: `0x08000000`,
`0x08002000`, `FLASH_OPTR`, `DBGMCU_IDCODE` and RAM. Halting the core
through `DHCSR` first returned `00000006` instead. `probe-rs download` still
worked, so the part could be flashed but not read, and `boot-matches.sh`
could not read the bootloader it was meant to check.

Reproduced by erasing the application's first page (an 8-byte `0xFF` image
at `0x08008000`): the same zeros. The same bootloader with `nop` in place
of `wfi` read back correctly in the same state: `0x08000000`
`20024000 080000c1`, `0x08008000` `ffffffff ffffffff`, `FLASH_OPTR`
`fffffeaa`. d837e43's bootloader spins. Why a sleeping core blocks the
probe's bus reads on this part was not established; the application also
sleeps in `wfi` between tasks and reads fine, after its HAL has configured
the clocks.

## The NOR image regions

Through the mailbox (`o89-dev erase-nor`): blocks 3712 and 3967, the first
and last of the image regions, were refused with `ImageRegion`; block 3711,
the ring's last, with `InsideTheRing`; blocks 3968 and 4095, above the
regions, were erased.

## What this does not show

The lines were not scoped. No update was staged or copied: that is M7. No
image reached past `0x08040000`, so programming and reading across the bank
boundary is still unproven on the part. RM0444's empty check was not read.
