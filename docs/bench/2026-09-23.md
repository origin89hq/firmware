## When a fresh slot is rolled back (#70)

#70 reports a slot flashed and then reset 6 to 10 s later coming back on the
factory image. Each run below flashed the slot, which leaves it `New`, waited
the delay from the moment `flash-comms` returned (the module's reset normal
is a 50 ms `EN` pulse that same moment), reset the module one of three ways,
and read which image the next boot loaded.

| Reset after the flash | Enable-pin reset through the bridge | Controller reflash, `just flash-controller` | Controller reset, `probe-rs reset`, #72's build `3894266` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | — | factory | — |
| 1 s | factory | — | — |
| 2 s | `ota_0` | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 4 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 5 s | `ota_0` | — | — |
| 6 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 10, 15, 20, 30 s | `ota_0` | — | — |

A rolled-back boot logs `Defaulting to factory image` and loads `0x10000`;
every other boot logged `Loaded app from partition at offset 0x210000`.

**The slot is at risk for under two seconds, which is the design.** The image
confirms its slot after its 1.5 s window (F-089), and a reset before that is
a pending slot at a reset, which the bootloader rolls back (F-088). A reset
from 2 s on, by any of the three routes and on either Embassy pin, found the
slot confirmed. The reflash at 0 s started as `flash-comms` returned; when in
the reflash the controller let go of the module's rail was not timed, only
that it came before the slot confirmed.

**#70's six to ten seconds were not reproduced.** Its delays were counted by
hand between commands, and its reflashes were `cargo run` and
`just flash-controller`, whose build and attach come before the reset; none
of its runs timed the reset itself. What this session shows is that nothing
in the ordinary images or either controller build holds the confirmation
back past its window.

Not done: the `otadata` bytes were not read back, only the bootloader's
choice; the bench images #70 first saw it with (`cuts`, `frames`) were not
repeated; and every reset here was one at a time, never a reset during the
confirmation's own write.
