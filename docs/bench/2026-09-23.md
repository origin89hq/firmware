# Bench session, 2026-09-23

Board A rev A, ST-LINK V2-1 `0671FF565357657287183750`, equipment
disconnected, no wire on the module, no analyser. Images built from the tree
of `2697836` (#73), clean source: controller 107 152 bytes, comms 112 896.
The comms image written into `ota_0` by the slot route through the
controller (`o89-dev flash-comms --layout slot`), by the knock, every time.
The bootloader's choice read from its own text through the bridge
(`o89-dev comms-listen`), whose reset the module reports as
`rst:0x1 (POWERON)`.

## When a fresh slot is rolled back (#70)

#70 reports a slot flashed and then reset 6 to 10 s later coming back on the
factory image. Each run below flashed the slot, which leaves it `New`; the
module's normal reset after the flash is a 50 ms `EN` pulse as `flash-comms`
returns. The run then waited the delay, started a command that resets the
module one of three ways, and read which image the next boot loaded.

**The delay is to the start of that command, and the reset lands after it.**
How long after differs by route, and only one route is timed:

- `probe-rs reset` of the controller finished 0.09 to 0.11 s after it
  started (three runs, timestamps before and after), so its rows are the
  reset to within a tenth of a second.
- The bridge's reset comes after the tool attaches and lands a mailbox
  request; that was not timed, so its rows are lower bounds.
- `just flash-controller` took 7.8 s from start to finish (four runs), its
  reset of the part at the end; when in it the controller let go of the
  module's rail was not timed, so its rows are lower bounds too.

| Command started after the flash | Enable-pin reset through the bridge | Controller reflash, `just flash-controller` | Controller reset, `probe-rs reset`, #72's build `3894266` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | — | factory | — |
| 1 s | factory | — | — |
| 2 s | `ota_0` | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 4 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 5 s | `ota_0` | — | — |
| 6 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 10, 15, 20, 30 s | `ota_0` | — | — |

A rolled-back boot logs `Defaulting to factory image` and loads `0x10000`;
every other boot logged `Loaded app from partition at offset 0x210000`.

**The slot is at risk for about two seconds, which is the design.** The
image confirms its slot after its 1.5 s window (F-089), and a reset before
that is a pending slot at a reset, which the bootloader rolls back (F-088).
The timed route bounds it: a controller reset within 2.1 s of the flash
found the slot confirmed. The bridge's reset rolled it back when started at
1 s. The other rows only say that a reset started at those delays, and
landing later, found the slot confirmed; the reflash started at 0 s reset
the module before the slot confirmed, at an instant not measured.

**#70's six to ten seconds were not reproduced.** Its delays were counted by
hand between commands, and its reflashes were `cargo run` and
`just flash-controller`, whose build and attach come before the reset; none
of its runs timed the reset itself. What this session shows is that nothing
in the ordinary images or either controller build holds the confirmation
back past its window.

Not done: the `otadata` bytes were not read back, only the bootloader's
choice; the bench images #70 first saw it with (`cuts`, `frames`) were not
repeated; and every reset here was one at a time, never a reset during the
confirmation's own write.

State left: the controller on the image from `2697836`'s tree (boot 157),
the module on the same tree's comms image in `ota_0`, confirmed, the link up.

## Dropping the ring's oldest block (#78, #80)

Same board and probe. Controller images from #80's branch, `6f3d385` and
then `621c1e1`, flashed over the bootloader with `just flash-controller`;
`o89-dev` from the same trees. Between runs and at the end, `main`'s
`279330a` (107 000 bytes).

**A ring block cannot be erased.** `o89-dev erase-nor 0` and `erase-nor 1`
answered *the block is the ring's; only the oldest goes, with `drop-ring`*,
and the ring read the same afterwards.

**The drop takes the oldest block and only it.** The ring held sequences 1
to 177, next 178, in ring blocks 42 and 43, where an earlier bench erase
had left its start. `o89-dev drop-ring --count 1`:

```text
dropped ring block 42; the oldest record is now 153
ring: next 178, oldest 153; newest first
```

The 25 records left read back 153 to 177 with no hole, the next sequence
unmoved. `o89-dev reboot` (boot 158 to 159) wrote its boot record at 178,
so no sequence came back, and the records of the power cut of 2026-09-22
§7 (163 to 165) are among those kept.

**An older firmware is refused, not trusted.** #80's `o89-dev` against
`main`'s controller: *the firmware's mailbox is version 2, this tool speaks
3*, before any request.

**The first fix of the oldest cost a second at boot, and the second does
not.** Time from `just flash-controller` returning to the first ping
answered, which includes the ring's open, on this ring of two blocks out of
3712:

| Controller | Mailbox up |
| --- | ---: |
| `main`, `279330a` | 0.90 s |
| `6f3d385`, the walk over every block for the oldest | 2.02 s |
| `621c1e1`, the walk only once the ring has wrapped | 0.89 s |

The difference is the walk from two past the head over the 3700 blocks a
young ring has never reached; `621c1e1` probes the last block once instead,
which is erased until the ring wraps. A page turn walks the same way, so
the second also left the recorder's appends.

Not done: the drop cut by a real power loss, which the host covers byte by
byte (`o89-sim`); and a ring that has wrapped, which this part's two blocks
are far from.

State left: `main`'s controller (boot 162), the ring holding 153 onward.

## The controller records' bodies on the ring (km43 0.4.1)

Same board and probe. Controller image from this branch at `9047433`
(108 848 bytes), flashed over the bootloader; the module on its ordinary
comms image in `ota_0`, the ESP-IDF v5.5.1 bootloader with its log.

After the flash and three `o89-dev reboot`s (boot 164 to 167), each boot
wrote its boot record and then the module's boot attempt as `comms boot
noise`:

```text
seq      190  A  0x0805  at -  comms boot noise: 4656 bytes that were not frames
seq      188  A  0x0805  at -  comms boot noise: 4656 bytes that were not frames
seq      186  A  0x0805  at -  comms boot noise: 4656 bytes that were not frames
seq      184  A  0x0805  at -  comms boot noise: 4656 bytes that were not frames
```

The same 4 656 bytes on every boot: the ROM's and the second-stage
bootloader's text at the link's baud, counted as bytes where 2026-09-19
counted about 1 140 refused runs of them. That is F-031's baseline in the
unit the record carries.

Not done: the ladder was not driven on the board, so `comms power cycled`
and `comms unrecoverable` with their bodies were read back only in the
simulator and the host tests, and no record failed its CRC to be summed.

State left: `main`'s controller.
