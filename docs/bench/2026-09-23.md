## When a fresh slot is rolled back (#70)

#70 reports a slot flashed and then reset 6 to 10 s later coming back on the
factory image. Each run below flashed the slot, which leaves it `New`; the
module's normal reset after the flash is a 50 ms `EN` pulse as `flash-comms`
returns. The run then waited the delay, started a command that resets the
module one of three ways, and read which image the next boot loaded.

**The delay is to the start of that command, and the reset lands after it.**
How long after differs by route, and only one route is timed:

- `probe-rs reset` of the controller finished 0.09 to 0.11 s after it
  started (three runs, timestamps before and after), so its rows are the
  reset to within a tenth of a second.
- The bridge's reset comes after the tool attaches and lands a mailbox
  request; that was not timed, so its rows are lower bounds.
- `just flash-controller` took 7.8 s from start to finish (four runs), its
  reset of the part at the end; when in it the controller let go of the
  module's rail was not timed, so its rows are lower bounds too.

| Command started after the flash | Enable-pin reset through the bridge | Controller reflash, `just flash-controller` | Controller reset, `probe-rs reset`, #72's build `3894266` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | — | factory | — |
| 1 s | factory | — | — |
| 2 s | `ota_0` | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 4 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 5 s | `ota_0` | — | — |
| 6 s | — | `ota_0` | `ota_0` |
| 10, 15, 20, 30 s | `ota_0` | — | — |

A rolled-back boot logs `Defaulting to factory image` and loads `0x10000`;
every other boot logged `Loaded app from partition at offset 0x210000`.

**The slot is at risk for about two seconds, which is the design.** The
image confirms its slot after its 1.5 s window (F-089), and a reset before
that is a pending slot at a reset, which the bootloader rolls back (F-088).
The timed route bounds it: a controller reset within 2.1 s of the flash
found the slot confirmed. The bridge's reset rolled it back when started at
1 s. The other rows only say that a reset started at those delays, and
landing later, found the slot confirmed; the reflash started at 0 s reset
the module before the slot confirmed, at an instant not measured.

**#70's six to ten seconds were not reproduced.** Its delays were counted by
hand between commands, and its reflashes were `cargo run` and
`just flash-controller`, whose build and attach come before the reset; none
of its runs timed the reset itself. What this session shows is that nothing
in the ordinary images or either controller build holds the confirmation
back past its window.

Not done: the `otadata` bytes were not read back, only the bootloader's
choice; the bench images #70 first saw it with (`cuts`, `frames`) were not
repeated; and every reset here was one at a time, never a reset during the
confirmation's own write.
